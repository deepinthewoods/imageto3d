Mix and match layers
Each layer is cut from its own generated model (its garment and its own skin) and stitched onto the game body's fixed neck / elbow / knee rings, so any armour, gloves and boots of a race join there. "bare" is the game body's own skin for that part. An outfit is the whole body below the neck (hands and feet included), so gloves and boots are off with an outfit.
