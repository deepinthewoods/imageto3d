## 3. Replaces `## Pipeline`

```markdown
## Pipeline

1. **Concept** (optional when a usable image already exists). One-shot image of the monster in the house style, **already in the
   marker colours**, on a transparent background, with its `colours.json`, saved as `concepts/<Folder>/codex<N>-front.png` with
   `codex<N>-front.prompt.json` (and `.colours.json`). Generated by Codex. Only these final files show in the app; `_attemptK`
   files, numbers with JSON but no PNG and loose legacy PNGs are hidden.
2. **Pick (the user, in the app).** The concept shows in the app's Concepts tab with a Monster tag (Provisional / Approve / Reject /
   Archive, like outfits; the stage is recorded in `concepts/<Folder>/.v2_state.json`, no file moves). **Approve** is the pick:
   the app gives it the next variant number and copies the concept into `pipeline/<Monster>/views/<v>/` (see "Folder layout").
   The approve dialog has a **convert straight away** tick (`unattended`). Agents never pick on the user's behalf.
3. **Extra views (automatic).** The app queues three Codex jobs from `views/<v>/front.png`: `left.png`, `back.png` and `right.png`
   (camera about 90° to the creature's left, 180° behind, 90° to its right, same height; prompts in
   `../../imageto3d/tools/preview/v2/monster_prompts.py`). No framing, pose or colour checks and no aesthetic retries (user,
   2026-11-25); one retry only for a missing or invalid file.
4. **Choose views (the user, in the app).** The Approved modal shows the four images with an **Enabled** tick each; **Use enabled
   views** freezes the list (`views.json` `frozen`, with sha256 per file) and queues the conversion. With `unattended` the freeze
   happens by itself with every available view once the view tasks finish.
5. **Convert (automatic, paid).** The app's `monster_convert` lane (one at a time): Hyper3D single image for one enabled view,
   multi-view (`hyper3d_multiview`) for 2 to 4, free preview, then the paid collect (0.5 credits, logged in imageto3d's
   `.codex_jobs/_app/paid_log.jsonl`), then `tools/reduce_monster.py` (five levels). It waits while conversions are paused or
   held and, in Auto mode, until the user is away (the Queue tab's **Run now** overrides the idle gate). It resumes from the
   record and never pays twice.
6. **Review (the user, in the app).** The variant reaches the 3D tab: pick the level and **Approve** (writes `chosen_lod.json`
   `{"lod", "approved": true}`); it then shows in Completed (**Unapprove** sends it back). **Reconvert** (paid, confirmed) is only
   offered in the 3D tab.
7. **Sections** (by hand, when parts of one colour must be colourable separately): `tools/split_monster_sections.py`. A card says
   `sections out of date` after a reconvert until it is run again.
8. **Rig** (by hand): `tools/rig_monster.py` on the approved level, then animate; see `RIGGING.md`. Library sync
   (`tools/build_monster_blends.py sync`) is by hand too.

Step 1 and the extra views are image generation; the conversion and reduction run in the app; picking, choosing views and approving
are the user's. Hand conversions with the wrappers (as before v2) still work but must not run on a variant the app is converting
(the record lock refuses a second command on the same output).
```

## Done means

A monster's conversion is finished when `pipeline/<Monster Name>/` holds the conversion image with `colours.json` and
`prompts.json`, `models/<variant>/hyper3d/` with an exported record and the GLB, and `models/<variant>/reduced/` with the five levels

`aaa ready/` or another category folder, rigging and library sync are separate steps.
